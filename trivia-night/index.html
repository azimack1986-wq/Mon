Why This Night Matters

Every year, thousands of Australian families face the heartbreak and fear of having a baby born too soon. This trivia night is our way of showing up for them.

Mon is running to raise money for premature babies and their families — supporting the tiniest fighters and the people who love them through some of the most challenging days of their lives. Your $20 donation buys you a seat at the table, a fun-filled evening, and the knowledge that you've made a real difference. Let's come together, share some laughs, and raise something beautiful.

About the Evening

A Night Built on Kindness

Gather your friends, family, or workmates and get ready for a night that's equal parts competitive and heartwarming. Whether you're a seasoned trivia champion or someone who confidently gets every answer wrong — you are so welcome here.

We'll have eight rounds of carefully crafted trivia covering everything from pop culture and history to sport and science. There'll be prizes, there'll be laughs, and there'll be a generous light supper to keep you fuelled through the final rounds.

Most of all, there'll be a room full of people who care. Every dollar raised goes directly toward supporting premature babies and their families through some of the hardest moments of their lives. That's worth showing up for.
